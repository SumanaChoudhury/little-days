# little days.
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Version:** 1.0  
**Product:** Cross-platform private memory and personal diary application  
**Platforms:** Android, iOS and Web  
**Visibility:** Strictly private, individual account  
**Status:** In Active Development

---

# 1. Product Vision

little days. is a private, cloud-based personal memory application that combines the familiar experience of social media with the functionality of a digital diary, scrapbook, photo archive and personal journal.

It allows users to document their lives through photographs, videos, stories, text, voice recordings and journal entries.

Unlike conventional social media, little days. has no public audience, follower system, engagement metrics or content discovery algorithm.

Its primary purpose is to preserve, organise and rediscover personal memories.

### Product mission

To create a beautiful, secure and permanent digital space where people can document their lives without worrying about public visibility, social pressure or losing their memories.

### Core principles

1. Privacy by default.
2. Original-quality media preservation.
3. Seamless cross-device access.
4. Offline accessibility.
5. Chronological memory organisation.
6. Long-term data portability.
7. Minimalist, personal and emotionally engaging design.
